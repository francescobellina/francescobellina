## About me

I am a physics master's student working at the interface between **statistical mechanics**, **computational biophysics** and **high-performance computing**. My goal is a **PhD in theoretical and computational biophysics**, where I want to understand biological systems through the same tools physics uses to explain matter: statistical ensembles, molecular dynamics and scalable numerical methods.

My coursework spans the theoretical, computational and experimental tracks, with a focus on **biophotonics and microscopy (SHG, TPE)**, **molecular dynamics** and **molecular statics**, and **machine learning for macromolecules**.

---

## Research interests

- **Computational biophysics:** molecular dynamics, molecular statics, neighbor lists, integrators (velocity Verlet)
- **Statistical mechanics:** ensembles, low-temperature Fermi gas, Sommerfeld expansion
- **Biophotonics & microscopy:** nonlinear optics, second-harmonic generation (SHG), two-photon excitation (TPE)
- **High-performance computing:** cache hierarchy, memory bandwidth, MPI, OpenMP
- **HPC for AI (advanced):** scaling deep learning workloads on HPC infrastructure, distributed training and efficient numerical kernels, explored in [**hpc4ai**](https://github.com/francescobellina/hpc4ai)
- **Optics & imaging:** off-axis digital holography and numerical reconstruction
